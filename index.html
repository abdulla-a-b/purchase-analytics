    Invoice matching

    PO · goods accepted · supplier invoice

    Matched = invoice quantity and price agree with the PO and with the quantity accepted at QC. Invoiced, goods pending = the invoice came before the goods (normal for LC imports) and is within the order. Mismatch = invoiced more than ordered (goods pending) or more than accepted (line closed), price outside tolerance, above the PO value, or paid beyond what was invoiced.
